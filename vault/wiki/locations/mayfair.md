# Mayfair

## 1. Context & Boundaries
Mayfair is a prestigious district in central London, known for its luxury shopping, historic architecture, and high-end dining. It spans from Oxford Street to Regent's Park, encompassing areas like Berkeley Square and Belgravia.

## 2. Pub Density & Characteristics
Characterized by elegant, historic pubs with a mix of traditional and contemporary decor. Many feature outdoor seating and curated beer selections.
