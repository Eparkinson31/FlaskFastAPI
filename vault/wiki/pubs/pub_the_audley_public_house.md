## Executive Summary
A historic pub in Mayfair offering a mix of traditional and modern dining, with a focus on British cuisine and live entertainment.

## History & Lore
Established in the 19th century, The Audley Public House has been a local landmark, known for its blend of classic charm and contemporary comforts.

## Architectural & Design Features
Features include period decor, outdoor seating, and a welcoming atmosphere with a piano player on weekends.

## Beverage & Food Menu
Specialties include scotch eggs, sausage rolls, bangers and mash, and a Sunday roast. The bar offers ales, bloody marys, and a selection of spirits.

## Logistics & Atmosphere
Outdoor seating available seasonally. Piano player performs on weekends. Popular for both casual dining and special occasions.
