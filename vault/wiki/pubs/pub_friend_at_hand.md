# Friend at Hand

Established in 2010, this traditional pub in Bloomsbury offers a welcoming atmosphere with modern amenities. Key features include:

- **Dogs allowed**
- **Live sports screenings**
- **Wine selection**
- **Outdoor seating**
- **Sunday roast**

The pub is known for its hearty meals like steak and ale pie and fish and chips, served with a side of ales from local breweries.
