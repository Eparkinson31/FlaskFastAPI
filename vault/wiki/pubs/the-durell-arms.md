# The Durell Arms

Located in Fulham, this pub offers a vibrant atmosphere with live music, karaoke, and sports screenings. Enjoy cocktails, wings, and hearty meals like steak and chips. The outdoor area is perfect for watching live sports, and the menu includes local beers like Camden and Guinness.
