# The Old City Arms

## Executive Summary
A traditional pub in Hammersmith with live music, outdoor seating, and a wide range of food options including Thai dishes and curries. Features include a resident cat, wine selection, and sports viewing areas.

## History & Significance
Established as a classic pub in the Hammersmith area, known for its community vibe and diverse menu.

## Notable Features
- **Live Music & Dancing**: Regular events with live performances.
- **Outdoor Seating**: Enjoy meals in the garden area.
- **Thai & Curries**: Specialties include authentic Thai dishes and rich curries.
- **Resident Cat**: A beloved cat named Mr. Whiskers is a regular visitor.
- **Sports Viewing**: Watch football matches on large screens.
- **Wine Selection**: Extensive wine list available by the glass or bottle.
