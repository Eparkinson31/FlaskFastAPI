# The York

## 1. Executive Summary
A traditional pub in Islington known for its outdoor seating, Sunday roasts, and football viewing.

## 2. History & Lore
Established in 1885 as a community hub for workers in the Islington textile district.

## 3. Architectural & Design Features
- **Layout:** Front bar with outdoor terrace, separate dining area.
- **Interior:** Exposed brick walls, original 19th-century wooden beams.
- **Key Artifacts:** Vintage football memorabilia on display.

## 4. Menu Highlights
- Sunday roast with all the trimmings
- Sausage and mash
- Traditional pies
- Cold pints of local ales
