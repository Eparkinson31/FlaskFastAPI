# The Marlborough

The Marlborough is a traditional pub located in Mayfair, offering a range of classic British dishes and beverages. Highlights include its wine selection, fish and chips, steak and ale pie, and a variety of ales. The pub also features outdoor seating and a menu with tiramisu, making it a popular spot for both locals and visitors.
