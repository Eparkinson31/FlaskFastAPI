# The Bloomsbury

Located in the heart of Bloomsbury, this traditional pub offers a charming blend of classic British hospitality and modern amenities. With its small pub size and stained glass windows, it provides a cozy atmosphere perfect for locals and visitors alike.

## Features
- **Outdoor Seating**: Enjoy drinks al fresco.
- **Live Music**: Regular performances add to the vibrant atmosphere.
- **Football & Screens**: Follow matches on multiple screens.
- **Beer & Ale Selection**: Includes Spitfire beer/ale, cask ale, Guinness, and a lager selection.
- **Food**: Serve fish and chips, fish finger sandwiches, pies, and more.
- **Wine Selection**: Offers a range of wines.
- **Specialty**: Traditional pub experience with a modern twist.

Perfect for a relaxed drink or a meal in a historic setting.
