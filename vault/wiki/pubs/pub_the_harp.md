## Summary
The Harp in Covent Garden offers real ale, craft beer, and cider with a vibrant atmosphere. Features include outdoor seating, bar games, and stained glass windows. A popular spot for London Pride events, known for its dark star hophead IPA and sausages. Located at 47 Chandos Pl, London WC2N 4HS.
